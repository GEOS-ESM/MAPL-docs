Subroutines
mapl_CollectiveGather3D mapl_CollectiveScatter3D

Provides collective 3-D scatter/gather operations that operate on ESMF_Grid objects. These require mapl_GridGetGlobal_mod (for global grid dimension queries) and the private request machinery from mapl_ShmemComms_mod, which is why they live in MAPL.geom rather than MAPL.esmf.
